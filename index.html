Behind the launch

What were VeoClara AI glasses like?

The pitch was simple: AI glasses that made litter and pollution disappear from your view. A polished frame, a product film and a futuristic interface made the idea feel like the next big launch.

It was an awareness stunt. The glasses and their dirt-removing software were fictional. We used the language of a tech launch to ask a real question: why imagine filtering out dirty streets instead of fixing them?

The reveal brings that promise back to reality. A cleaner view needs cleaner streets — and a shared commitment to making them that way.
